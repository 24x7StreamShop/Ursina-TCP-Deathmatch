Ursina TCP Deathmatch / Remote play

Play together, even from different cities.

Tailscale puts both computers on a private, encrypted network. Once you are connected, your friend joins the game using your Tailscale address, just as if you were on the same Wi-Fi.

!
Should you share the reusable link?

Yes, but send it privately and only to the person you trust. Anyone who gets a reusable invite may be able to join your Tailscale network, depending on your admin and access settings. Do not post it in a public chat, screenshot, stream, or repository. After your friend joins, remove or revoke the reusable invite in the Tailscale admin console.

01

Connect your friend

1

Send the invite privately

Send the Tailscale reusable-link URL directly to your friend. Tell them not to forward it. They open it, sign in or create a Tailscale account, and follow the join prompt.

2

Install Tailscale

Your friend installs Tailscale on the same computer where they will run the game, then signs in with the account that accepted the invite. The Tailscale icon should show that the connection is active.

3

Check the connection

Both of you should see the other device in Tailscale. You can test it from PowerShell with tailscale ping YOUR_TAILSCALE_IP.

02

Start the match

You are the host

On your computer, start play/server.exe. Keep its terminal window open. It will print a Tailscale IPv4 address beginning with 100., plus the port 8888.

Server address: 100.x.y.z
Port: 8888

Send the game address

Send your friend only the Tailscale IP shown by the server, such as 100.121.132.98. They should use this address, not your home Wi-Fi address and not your public IP.

Your friend joins

Your friend starts play/client.exe, enters your 100.x.y.z address, leaves the port at 8888, chooses a username, and clicks Play.

03

Keep the session tidy

Before playing

  • Tailscale is running on both computers.
  • Your friend accepted the invite on the account used by Tailscale.
  • You started the server before your friend starts the client.
  • Windows Firewall allows the server on private networks.

After playing

  • Close the server window to end the match.
  • Revoke the reusable invite if nobody else needs it.
  • Remove your friend from the tailnet when access should end.
  • Keep your Tailscale address and invite link private.
04

If it does not connect

  • Confirm the server is still running and listening on port 8888.
  • Confirm your friend entered the host's Tailscale 100.x.x.x address exactly.
  • Run tailscale status on both computers and check that both devices are online.
  • Run tailscale ping YOUR_TAILSCALE_IP from your friend's computer.
  • If the ping works but the game does not, allow server.exe through Windows Firewall and try again.
The game uses TCP port 8888. Tailscale removes the need for router port forwarding when both players are connected to the same tailnet.
