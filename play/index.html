Ursina TCP Deathmatch / Remote play

Play together, even from different cities.

Tailscale puts both computers on a private, encrypted network. Your friend can then join your game from another home without router port forwarding or public IP addresses.

!
Should you share the reusable link?

Yes, but send it privately and only to the person you trust. Anyone who gets a reusable invite may be able to join your Tailscale network, depending on your admin and access settings. Do not post it in a public chat, screenshot, stream, or repository. After your friend joins, remove or revoke the reusable invite in the Tailscale admin console.

First, install the app

Browser sign-in is only step one.

Your friend opens the invite in a browser, accepts it, and then installs Tailscale on the same laptop where they will run the game. The Tailscale desktop app must be open and connected while playing.

01

Connect your friend

1

Send the invite privately

Send the reusable-link URL directly to your friend. Tell them not to forward it. They open it, sign in or create a Tailscale account, and follow the join prompt.

2

Accept in the browser

After signing in, your friend accepts the invitation. Browser sign-in alone does not create the game connection; it only gives their account permission to join your tailnet.

3

Open the desktop app

Your friend installs Tailscale, opens it, and signs in with the same account. The tray icon should say it is connected. Leave the app running while client.exe is open.

Quick check: both devices should appear as online in Tailscale. From PowerShell, test with tailscale ping YOUR_TAILSCALE_IP.

02

Start the match

You host the game

  • Open Tailscale and confirm it is connected.
  • Run play/server.exe.
  • Keep the server window open.
Tailscale IP: 100.x.y.z
Port: 8888
→

Your friend joins

  • Run play/client.exe.
  • Enter your 100.x.y.z address.
  • Use port 8888, choose a username, and click Play.

Send the Tailscale IP printed by your server, not your home Wi-Fi address and not your public IP. The two computers do not need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

03

Keep the session tidy

Before playing

  • Tailscale is running on both computers.
  • Your friend accepted the invite on the account used by Tailscale.
  • You started the server before your friend starts the client.
  • Windows Firewall allows the server on private networks.

After playing

  • Close the server window to end the match.
  • Revoke the reusable invite if nobody else needs it.
  • Remove your friend from the tailnet when access should end.
  • Keep your Tailscale address and invite link private.
04

If it does not connect

  • Confirm the server is still running and listening on port 8888.
  • Confirm your friend entered the host's Tailscale 100.x.x.x address exactly.
  • Run tailscale status on both computers and check that both devices are online.
  • Run tailscale ping YOUR_TAILSCALE_IP from your friend's computer.
  • If the ping works but the game does not, allow server.exe through Windows Firewall and try again.
The game uses TCP port 8888. Tailscale removes the need for router port forwarding when both players are connected to the same tailnet.
